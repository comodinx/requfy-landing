Are my projects uploaded to a server?

No. They live on your phone. They only go to your repository if you connect Git.

How do I sync with Git?

In the project settings, choose "Connect to Git" and use a GitHub token with access to that repository.

How do I change the language or the theme?

In the Settings tab.
