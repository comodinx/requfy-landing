Free · In Spanish and English · Light and dark

Everything you need to test an API, in your pocket

  • Git without conflicts

    Sync with your repo: requfy merges changes field by field and, if both sides touched the same thing, what you did on your phone wins.

  • Bruno format

    Your collections are .bru files in your repo. Keep editing them with Bruno on your computer.

  • Environments and secrets

    Switch servers in one tap. Secret variables are never pushed to the repo.

  • Clear responses

    Status, time and size at a glance, JSON as a tree and the history of every run.

  • Chained variables

    Take a value from one response and use it in the next request, or build a URL from other variables.

  • Private

    No ads, no tracking. Your requests go straight from your phone to your APIs.

Bruno compatible

requfy reads and writes collections in the Bruno format (.bru files). It is an independent project: Bruno is a trademark of Anoop M D, and requfy is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bruno.
