Delete your account

Last updated: October 23, 2026

From the app

Go to Account › Delete account and confirm. If you signed in with email, we ask for your password; if you signed in with Google or Apple, you need to sign in again.

Without the app

Email [PENDIENTE: email de contacto] from your account email, with the subject "Delete my requfy account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

The account is deleted. On the device, its projects, environments, history and Git connections are deleted, along with their tokens.

Your GitHub repositories are not touched.

Projects from a guest session stay on the device.
